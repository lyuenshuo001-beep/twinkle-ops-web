# TWINKLE OPS — Asset credits and modifications

All listed resources are stored locally. This game contains no resources extracted from other commercial games.

* M4A1 — 3DModelsCC0, submitted by nisu. CC0. https://opengameart.org/content/m4a1-assault-rifle . URP material conversion and original charging-handle animation.
* AKM — LonesomeDucky. CC0. https://opengameart.org/content/akm . Blender-to-FBX conversion and URP materials.
* Unfinished Buildings — loafbrr_1. CC0. https://opengameart.org/content/unfinished-buildings . Selected structures arranged into a quarantine construction district; collision-only meshes hidden.
* Infected worker model — Pixelhouse. CC BY 3.0, https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/ . https://opengameart.org/content/zombie . Author website: http://www.pixelhouse.com.ar . Changes: scale, URP materials, humanoid retargeting.
* Infected animations — Rikindle3D. CC0. https://opengameart.org/content/male-city-zombie-ready-for-use-in-game-engines . Humanoid retargeting and animation state machine. The original burned character mesh is not used in the raid.
* First-person arms — para / MakeHuman team. CC0. https://opengameart.org/content/fps-arms-rigged-only . URP materials and original procedural poses.
* Concrete Road Barrier — Amal Kumar; Barrel 03 — Serhii Khromov; Old Military Crate — Jack Mava; Asphalt 02 — Rob Tuytel. Poly Haven, CC0. https://polyhaven.com/license . Individual sources: https://polyhaven.com/a/concrete_road_barrier , https://polyhaven.com/a/barrel_03 , https://polyhaven.com/a/old_military_crate , https://polyhaven.com/a/asphalt_02 . Changes: 2K texture selection, URP conversion, crate variant selection.
* Recorded AR-15 and AK-47 shots — Ben Jaszczak, Brian Nelson, Kevin Heras, Matthew Nanney. CC0. https://opengameart.org/content/the-free-firearm-sound-library . AR-15 trimmed single shot; AK-47 changed in V1.1 to the isolated final discharge of C_27P, eliminating the mistakenly included long burst. Mono 48kHz, normalized. AR-15 recording accompanies the M4A1 model.
* Rifle reload — SpringySpringo. CC0. https://opengameart.org/content/gun-reload-sounds . Recorded using airsoft equipment.
* Wind loop — SketchMan3, derived from JaggedStone's Loopable Dungeon Ambience. CC0. https://opengameart.org/content/wind-whoosh-loop and https://opengameart.org/content/loopable-dungeon-ambience .
* Noto Sans SC — Google / Noto contributors. SIL Open Font License 1.1; accompanying OFL.txt. The UI subset is modified and renamed Twinkle Ops SC; unused glyphs removed for Web delivery.
* Item UI illustrations — original project artwork created for TWINKLE OPS.

Download source pages, SHA-256 hashes and original archives are retained under SourceAssets/OpenGameArt and SourceAssets/PolyHaven on the local drive. Only redistributable converted assets are intended for Git LFS.

## V1.1 additions

* M9 Knife PBR — khairul169, CC0. https://opengameart.org/content/m9-knife-pbr . Original model and PBR maps, resized and converted to URP; original procedural first-person swing.
* Remington 870 model — MikeMoon, CC0. https://opengameart.org/content/shotgun-1 . Model converted to FBX, UVs repacked, original project wood/steel texture variations baked at 1K. The model is a lightweight asset; it does not include a professionally captured reload animation.
* Shotgun recording — Free Firearm Sound Library authors credited above, CC0. Model 12/K_22P.wav, isolated 1.1-second discharge, normalized mono. This is a Winchester Model 12 recording used with the M870 model, not a claim that it was recorded from an M870.
* Melee swish — remaxim, based on qubodup's bamboo-stick swishes, CC0. https://opengameart.org/content/3-melee-sounds . Volume balanced for the knife.
* Metal Trash Can — GurJas Studios; Metal Toolbox — Mateusz Sadek; Vintage Suitcase — Maximilian Schuster; Trashbag — Benny Weimer; Old Bed Frame — Luca B; Shelf 01 — Gabriel Radić; Tool Cart — Savva Zakharov. Poly Haven, CC0, https://polyhaven.com/license . Individual assets: https://polyhaven.com/a/metal_trash_can , https://polyhaven.com/a/metal_toolbox , https://polyhaven.com/a/vintage_suitcase , https://polyhaven.com/a/trashbag , https://polyhaven.com/a/old_bed_frame , https://polyhaven.com/a/Shelf_01 , https://polyhaven.com/a/tool_cart . 1K PBR textures, URP conversion, bounds/collision setup. Source API metadata and checksums in Assets/ThirdParty/V11/SOURCES.json.
* Metal safe with combination lock — johndh. Chosen license: **Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 3.0 Unported**, https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/3.0/ . Source: https://opengameart.org/content/metal-safe-with-combination-lock . Changes: mesh conversion, UV atlas and weathered steel texture bake. The safe model, its adapted mesh and safe-specific textures remain under CC BY-SA 3.0; this does not relicense unrelated game code/assets. Original source is provided in Docs/AssetSources/Safe.blend, alongside the reproducible conversion script in Tools/BakeV11Assets.py. Do not remove this attribution or apply additional restrictions to these safe assets.

V1.1 infected variants derive from the credited Pixelhouse worker: weighted head-mesh proportions, stature and material tints. They retain CC BY 3.0 attribution. These are variants of one base character, not independently authored character models.
